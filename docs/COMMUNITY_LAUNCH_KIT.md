## Hacker News

**Title:** Show HN: Open-source MCP for evidence-led marketing decisions

**URL:** https://github.com/Gourav2411/general-marketing-intelligence

**First comment:**

I built General Marketing Intelligence because my marketing data kept living in separate tools while the actual decision still happened in spreadsheets, meetings and memory.

It is a local-first, open-source MCP server for Claude and Codex. It connects GA4, Search Console, Google Ads, CRM and CSV evidence, then exposes 62 tools across executive review, acquisition, content, measurement and governance.

The part I care about is the boundary between data and reasoning:

- calculations and joins happen in code;
- every important numeric claim can carry provenance;
- a nine-evaluator council makes disagreement visible;
- account learning is derived from recorded outcomes, not model memory;
- write actions require preview, explicit approval and a separate execution request;
- deterministic behavior remains available without an LLM API key.

The local transport is stdio. A separate Streamable HTTP foundation adds OAuth verification, tenant isolation, encrypted credentials, origin checks, rate limits and audit records. It is not presented as a hosted SaaS yet.

I am looking for blunt feedback on the tool surface, evidence model, MCP ergonomics and whether the human-approval design is understandable. I am also inviting contributors for connector testing and editorial review of the governed marketing-case corpus.

**Maker comment:**

I started building GMI to solve a problem I had as a marketing leader: every platform had a dashboard, but none held the complete decision.

GMI brings marketing evidence into Claude and Codex through MCP. It can connect search, analytics, paid media and CRM data, then turn that evidence into executive reviews, channel diagnostics, experiment plans and monitored decisions.

It deliberately separates three things that AI products often blur:

1. Data retrieval and calculations happen in code.
2. Strategic reasoning is governed by evidence, provenance and account learning.
3. Humans remain the final approvers for consequential actions.

It is open source because I want practitioners, RevOps teams, engineers and researchers to test it, challenge it and improve it instead of buying another disconnected dashboard.
